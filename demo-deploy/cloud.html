❓ FAQ

Common Questions

Can I subscribe to individual modules or only the bundle? ▼
Both. Subscribe to any individual module (NetMon, SentryLog, Citadel Backup, AI Agent, Dev Environment) separately, or get the Full Suite Bundle at a 20% discount. Mix and match anytime.
How is my data kept separate from other clients? ▼
Each client gets a fully isolated container with dedicated compute, storage, and networking. There are zero shared data paths between tenants. Your data is encrypted at rest with keys only you control.
Do I need any hardware? ▼
No. Everything runs on MyClover.Tech cloud infrastructure. All you need is an internet connection and a modern browser. For hybrid setups, we also offer the Citadel NAS appliance for on-premises nodes.
What does the AI Agent actually do? ▼
Your private AI agent can monitor your infrastructure, analyze logs, generate reports, troubleshoot issues, automate routine IT tasks, create documentation, and respond to alerts — all without human intervention. It runs 24/7 on our infrastructure.
Can I cancel or change my subscription? ▼
Yes. Subscriptions are month-to-month. Add or remove modules anytime. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle. No long-term contracts, no cancellation fees.
Is this suitable for MSPs managing multiple clients? ▼
Absolutely. The Full Suite Bundle includes MSP white-label branding. You get a multi-tenant management dashboard, per-client billing, and the ability to set your own margins. Volume discounts available for 10+ seats.
Where is the data stored? ▼
All data is stored on MyClover.Tech-managed infrastructure with triple-redundant backups: local primary, local mirror, and encrypted offsite cloud. We never store data on third-party consumer cloud services.
What's the uptime guarantee? ▼
We provide a 99.9% uptime SLA for all cloud modules. Our triple-redundant infrastructure ensures that even if a node fails, your services continue uninterrupted.
